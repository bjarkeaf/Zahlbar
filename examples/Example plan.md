# Example plan
%% Format: https://github.com/bjarkeaf/Zahlbar/blob/main/FORMAT.md %%

A made-up yearly plan for someone paid partly in company shares. Amounts are before tax, and the tax on shares is paid from cash.

## Income
- Base salary: 7 500
- Stock grant vest: 5 500 quarterly {shares}
- Share discount: 2 000 half-yearly {shares}
- Employer pension: 750 {pension}
- Holiday pay: 1 300 yearly

## Tax
- Income tax: 2 700
- Tax on stock grant: 2 250 quarterly
- Tax on share discount: 800 half-yearly
- Tax on holiday pay: 550 yearly
- Top tax (estimate): 1 200 yearly

## Saving
- Share purchase plan: 1 850 {shares}
- Own pension: 375 {pension}
- Sell vested shares: -2 000 quarterly {shares}

## Living
- Housing: 700
- Leisure and travel: 650
- Transport: 250
- Clothes and personal care: 150
- Other: 150
- Groceries: 100
- Insurance: 90
- Lunch at work: 65
- Subscriptions: 60
- Haircut: 40 every 6 weeks
- ~~Car: 400~~
